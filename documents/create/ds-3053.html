DS-3053 guide

Fill out Form DS-3053

Complete sections 1 through 3 of the U.S. Department of State’s child passport consent form. Sign section 4 when the notary or passport officer asks you to.

Signing from Haiti or another country? Check State Department rules before booking.

1. Child’s information
Enter last name first, then first and middle names as they appear on Form DS-11.

For ages 16 or 17, use this form if the passport office asks for it. Section 2 stays blank.

2. Adult applying with the child
Enter last name first, then first and middle names.
3. Parent or guardian giving consent

Enter the details of the legal parent or guardian who will be absent from the passport appointment.

Second parent or guardian

Your details stay in this browser while you fill out the form. The PDF is sent securely when you choose to continue to notarization.

Review your DS-3053

Wait for the notary before signing. For the passport application, print the notarized form and include a copy of the front and back of the photo ID each signer presents to the notary.

If both parents or guardians will sign, add both signers to the notarization.
