When is Standard available?

Standard support is available Monday–Friday, 9 AM–8 PM, and Saturday, 10 AM–5 PM Eastern. Closed Sundays.

Can I request a notary after hours?

Yes. On-Demand requests are available 24/7. Connection time depends on notary availability.

What do I need for the session?

Your completed document, valid government-issued photo ID, and a phone or computer with a camera and microphone. Sign when the notary instructs you.

Are there any additional fees?

Standard starts at $25 for 1 signer and 2 stamps. On-Demand starts at $30 for 1 signer and 1 stamp. See add-on prices above for extra signers, stamps, or witnesses. International Standard sessions start at $35.

Can I bring my own document?

Yes. Use your completed document, or choose a form or template from our document library. Confirm that the receiving organization accepts online notarization.
